Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the competition

Who is eligible to participate?

All currently enrolled LAUTECH students from any faculty or department are eligible. Both undergraduate and postgraduate students can participate. Teams must consist of 2-4 students currently enrolled at LAUTECH.

What are the key dates and deadlines?

Key dates include: Registration closes on January 30, 2026. Bootcamps run in November, December, and January. Preliminary submissions due January 31, Semi-finals on February 10, and Campus Pitch Day on February 25, 2026.

What resources are available for participants?

Participants receive access to three bootcamps, mentorship from industry experts, networking opportunities with past winners and judges, resources on UN Sustainable Development Goals, and personalized feedback on proposals.

Is there a registration fee?

No, participation in Hult Prize LAUTECH 2026 is completely free for all LAUTECH students. All bootcamps, resources, and mentorship are provided at no cost to participants.
